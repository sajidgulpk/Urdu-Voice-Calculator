1. Introduction & Overview

  • Commitment: Developed by Sajid AI Lab, the app is fully committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring personal information is safe in compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, GDPR, and global data protection standards.
  • Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee: The core calculator and learning features operate locally. However, to support free availability, the app integrates Google AdMob advertising services as described below.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

All calculations, voice processing, and settings are performed locally on the device:

  • Local Calculation History: Equations and results are saved securely in a local database (SQLite / Room Database) on your smartphone. This data never leaves your device and can be deleted anytime using the "Clear History" button.
  • Voice Recognition (Speech Input): Voice input for numbers/formulas is handled through standard Android speech services. Voice clips are neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS Voice Output): Results spoken in Urdu, Arabic, English, or Hindi use the device's built-in Android Text-To-Speech engine. No audio data is uploaded to any server.
  • App Preferences: Selected themes (3D Cards, Classic, Neo, Emerald), speech speed, pitch, and language choices are stored locally via Android SharedPreferences / DataStore.

3. Third-Party Services & Google AdMob Advertising

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements within the app. AdMob may use device identifiers, advertising ID, cookies, and similar technologies to collect information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and measure ad performance.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: You can learn more about how Google uses data for advertising by visiting Google’s Privacy & Terms.
  • Ad Personalization / Opt-Out: Users can manage or opt-out of personalized advertising through their device settings (e.g., Google Settings -> Ads -> Reset advertising ID or Opt out of personalized ads).

4. Device Permissions Required

To deliver core features and display ads, the app may request standard Android permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO / Microphone: Used exclusively when tapping the microphone button for voice-guided calculations. Audio is processed transiently and never recorded or saved.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for tactile haptic feedback when pressing buttons on the calculator keypad.
  • INTERNET: Required for displaying advertisements via Google AdMob SDK.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

  • Includes educational learning modules (Urdu Counting 1-100, Multiplication Tables 1-20, and Place Values) suitable for students and children.
  • Practices: Does not collect personal information from children under 13; ads served comply with Google Play's Families Policy and child-directed ad treatment where applicable.

6. Data Security

  • Because core calculation data remains exclusively on the local device, security is protected by standard Android operating system sandboxing.
  • Uninstalling the application completely removes all locally stored calculation history and preferences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • The policy may be periodically updated to reflect future updates, legal compliance, or advertising network requirements.
  • Revisions will be published with an updated effective date on the official page.
