1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Urdu Voice Calculator (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Sajid AI Lab. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is safe.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data, permissions, and device storage in compliance with the Google Play Developer Policy, GDPR, and other global data protection standards.

🔒 Zero Personal Data Collection Guarantee

Urdu Voice Calculator operates 100% offline. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal identity information, mathematical calculations, voice recordings, or history to any external servers or third parties.

2. Information We Process & How It Is Handled

The App performs all calculations, voice processing, and settings locally on your device:

  • Local Calculation History: Your calculation equations and results are saved securely in a local database on your device (SQLite / Room Database). This data never leaves your smartphone and can be deleted by you at any time with the "Clear History" button.
  • Voice Recognition (Speech Input): When you use the voice input feature to dictate numbers or formulas, speech-to-text processing is handled through standard Android speech services. We do not store or transmit your voice clips.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS Voice Output): When the app speaks results in Urdu, Arabic, English, or Hindi, it uses your device's built-in Android Text-To-Speech engine. No audio data is uploaded to any server.
  • App Preferences: Your selected theme (3D Cards, Classic, Neo, Emerald), speech speed, pitch, and language choices are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences / DataStore.

3. Device Permissions Required

To deliver its core features, Urdu Voice Calculator may request the following standard Android permissions:

  • RECORD_AUDIO / Microphone: Used exclusively when you tap the microphone button to perform voice-guided calculations. Audio is processed transiently and is never recorded or saved.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback when you press buttons on the calculator keypad.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application includes educational learning modules (Urdu Counting 1-100, Multiplication Tables 1-20, and Place Values) suitable for students and children. We strictly uphold children's privacy standards:

  • We do not collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • The App contains no behavioral tracking, profiling, or data harvesting.
  • All educational content is safe, offline, and family-friendly.

5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Urdu Voice Calculator does not use any third-party analytics trackers, behavioral advertising SDKs, or background location monitors. Your activity inside the app is completely private to you.

6. Data Security

Since all data remains exclusively on your local device, its security is protected by standard Android operating system sandboxing. Uninstalling the application completely removes all locally stored calculation history and preferences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect future updates or legal compliance. Any revisions will be published with an updated effective date on this page.
